## Overview

At Schneider Electric's AI Hub / Energy Simulation Team, I engineered Model Predictive Control (MPC) algorithms in MATLAB/Simulink for bidirectional (Vehicle‑to‑Grid / Vehicle‑to‑Building) EV charging within microgrids.

## What it does

- Formulates and solves an MPC problem that dispatches EV charge/discharge power alongside other microgrid assets in real time.
- Includes a battery degradation model based on weighted Ah‑throughput, used to bias dispatch decisions toward longer battery life.

## Outcomes

- Demonstrated technical and economic feasibility of bidirectional EV charging in microgrids.
- Reduced simulation time by **85%** while maintaining **99.8% accuracy** for real‑time applications.
- Extended battery lifespan by **2+ years** in Vehicle‑to‑Building scenarios through optimized charging strategies.
