## The Journey

As Team Leader for powertrain, I assembled a diverse group of engineering students with a bold mission: build Pakistan's first formula-style electric race car from the ground up. We faced an ambitious challenge—sourcing components, designing custom systems, and assembling a high-performance vehicle that could compete on the global stage. Every decision mattered, every optimization counted, and every team member's expertise became essential. The passion was palpable as we transformed sketches and simulations into reality, night after night, test after test.

### 🏆  1: Engineering Excellence

We engineered a high-performance drivetrain capable of speeds exceeding 100 mph, featuring a bespoke motor controller and a high-density battery pack with advanced regenerative braking. Through iterative testing and relentless refinement, we optimized every subsystem for maximum efficiency and reliability. The result was a machine that didn't just meet specifications, it exceeded them, proving that precision engineering and innovative design could flourish outside Silicon Valley or Detroit.

### 🏆 2: Global Recognition

Our vehicle earned a **3rd place finish in the Design Challenge** at the Formula Student UK competition, an event that draws the brightest engineering teams from around the world. This achievement resonated far beyond a trophy—it placed Pakistan on the international motorsports engineering map and demonstrated our technical prowess to a global audience. We proved that world-class innovation knows no geographic boundaries.

### 🏆 3: Inspiration & Legacy

This project became a testament to our ability to conceptualize, deliver, and showcase innovative engineering solutions on the global stage. We didn't just build a race car; we inspired a generation of Pakistani engineers to dream bigger and reach higher. The journey highlighted Pakistan's growing capabilities in electric motorsports engineering and opened doors for future innovation in sustainable transportation technology. Years later the team continues to thrive.
