In the spring of 2023, Wajih and I, supported by grants from the US Alumni project and other generous sources, embarked on a community service initiative in Skardu. In collaboration with a local NGO, we established a public day care center that enabled women to pursue work and leisure activities. We also upgraded a craft-making facility to support individuals with disabilities in creating and selling their crafts. This project has been deeply meaningful to us, and we are grateful for the opportunity to have contributed to the community.
