    In the summer of 2022, I joined the debate team at Kansas State University during what turned out to be one of their strongest debate years ever. We were constantly traveling across the country, qualifying for tournament after tournament, each one pushing us closer to greatness. Our journey took us all the way to Seattle in the cold, snowy December (the very same week K State won the Big 12 Football Championship, by the way). At that tournament, my teammate and I made it to the final round, where we earned the silver cup and the title of Best BP Debaters, competing against 53 teams from all across North America.
