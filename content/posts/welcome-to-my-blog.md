## Why this site exists

I wanted a simple, fast, and fully-owned place to write about the things I build and experience &mdash; without relying on a third-party platform. This site is built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page/) theme, and it's hosted for free on GitHub Pages.

## What to expect

- Write-ups on engineering projects
- Lessons learned from work and personal experiments
- Occasional notes on tools, workflows, and things I find interesting

More posts coming soon.
